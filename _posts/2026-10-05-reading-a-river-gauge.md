The staff gauge is one of the simplest instruments in water: a marked board fixed to a bridge or wall, read by eye. It's also the visual motif of this site — look at the top of the home page.

## Stage is not flow

A gauge tells you **stage**, the height of the water surface. What we usually want is **discharge**, the volume passing per second. The link between the two is the *rating curve*, built from repeated flow measurements at different levels.

## Why it matters

- Flood warnings depend on reliable stage readings.
- Abstraction licences are often tied to flow thresholds.
- Long records let us see how catchments respond to land-use and climate change.

A painted post, read consistently for decades, becomes one of the most valuable datasets a catchment has.
