Water is one of those fields where the most important work is invisible when it goes well. Nobody notices the treatment works that ran smoothly overnight or the main that didn't burst. I want to write about that hidden work: how it's done, why it's hard, and how we could do it better.

## What you'll find here

- **Technical notes** on treatment, networks and wastewater, written for practitioners.
- **Reflections** on policy, regulation and how the sector is changing.
- **Course updates** — I'm building an online course for water professionals, and I'll share how it's going.

## A note on format

Posts are written in Markdown. That means headings start with `##`, lists start with `-`, and **bold** uses two asterisks. You can add images too:

```
![Description of the image](/assets/images/your-photo.jpg)
```

> Tip: quotes like this one start with a `>` symbol.
